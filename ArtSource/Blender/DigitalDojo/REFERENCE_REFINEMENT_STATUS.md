## Saved Unity changes

`TargetMountMotion` now waits through an 80 ms preload and finishes deployment at 700 ms. It starts the visual against its support, swings the side target into its final orientation, and places three overlapping telescopic sleeves. Movement and matching remain blocked until deployment finishes. Pausing uses scaled time. Reduced motion removes displacement while retaining the timing gate.

Accepted and below-strength hits have distinct recoil amplitudes. Below-strength hits do not consume targets or unlock deployment. Partial heavy kick effects use the kick colour. `DigitalDojoTargetVisual.BeginBreak` moves named authored fragments deterministically, preserving their original transforms. The new fragment meshes are not yet in the imported Unity prefabs, so those prefabs cannot yet show that complete fracture effect.

Resolution bookkeeping and scheduled cleanup still precede optional fracture presentation. Missing or failed presentation cannot cancel the destruction deadline. The additional sleeves remain target-owned and have no colliders.
